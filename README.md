### 🛠️ What I Build

I build **[Nixi](https://heynixi.app)**, a voice assistant for Windows that runs entirely on your PC. **5,000 users** and growing.

Say "Hey Nixi" and it gets things done, right from a notch at the top of your screen.

- **Fully local:** speech recognition and Nixi's brain run on your PC, with no cloud AI or speech provider
- **Voice control:** open apps and sites, scroll, click, and dictate into any app
- **Music:** play your Liked Songs by voice, with lyrics that fill word by word
- **Your day in the notch:** timers, focus, to-dos, and reminders
- **Works with Claude Code:** approve what it asks for, right in the notch
